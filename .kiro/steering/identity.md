# Quadropus — Identity & Governance

Quadropus is the parent system. It is NOT just another product — it is the operating system for Fundy Logic Inc.

## What Quadropus IS

1. **The governance layer** — defines quality, security, and architecture standards across ALL products
2. **The client-facing brand** — delivers websites, SEO, and AI tools to business clients
3. **The decision authority** — when there's a conflict between speed and quality, Quadropus rules apply

## Quadropus Rules

1. Every product ships with auth, rate limiting, and cost protection BEFORE launch
2. Every product has CURRENT_STATUS.md and TODO.md maintained
3. Every product has project-level steering for its domain-specific rules
4. The global overwatch (`~/.kiro/steering/quadropus-overwatch.md`) cannot be overridden by project-level steering
5. Security rules are inherited, not duplicated — project steering adds specifics, never relaxes the global standard
6. When in doubt, the answer is: protect the user, protect the business, then move fast
